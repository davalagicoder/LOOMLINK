# LOOMLINK
### Connecting Traditional Artisans and Weavers to Digital Markets

**Team Name:** Alpha Coders  
**Hackathon:** TECH4BHARAT 2026

## 1. About the Project
LOOMLINK is a digital marketplace concept that connects traditional artisans and weavers directly with shopkeepers and bulk buyers. It aims to improve market access, encourage teamwork, and promote fair opportunities for artisan communities.

## 2. Problem Statement
Traditional artisans often face challenges such as limited market access, dependence on middlemen, lack of bulk orders, and difficulties coordinating group work.

## 3. Our Solution
LOOMLINK provides a common digital platform where artisans can form groups, showcase handmade products, connect with shopkeepers, and manage bulk-order opportunities.

## 4. Key Features
- User registration with different roles
- Artisan group creation and management
- Democratic leader nomination and voting
- Handmade product marketplace
- Bulk-order requests from shopkeepers
- Work allocation among artisan group members
- Transparent payment distribution
- Planned AI-assisted product descriptions and regional language support

## 5. Proposed Payment Model
The proposed payment distribution is:
- 95% – Artisan group
- 5% – Group leader coordination commission

This is an illustrative proposed model for our concept.

## 6. How LOOMLINK Works
1. Artisans register on the platform.
2. Artisans create or join groups.
3. Group members select their leader.
4. Artisans showcase their products.
5. Shopkeepers place bulk-order requests.
6. The group leader coordinates work allocation.
7. Payments are distributed transparently.

## 7. Expected Impact
- Better market access for artisans
- Support for traditional craftsmanship
- More opportunities for group collaboration
- Improved transparency
- Support for sustainable livelihoods
