About Our Idea

LOOMLINK is a digital marketplace concept designed to connect traditional artisans and weavers directly with shopkeepers and bulk buyers. Our aim is to reduce dependency on middlemen and create better market opportunities for artisan communities.

The Problem

Many traditional artisans face challenges such as limited market access, dependence on intermediaries, lack of bulk-order opportunities and difficulties coordinating their work as a group.

Our Proposed Solution

LOOMLINK proposes a collaborative digital platform where artisans can form groups, showcase handmade products, connect with shopkeepers and coordinate bulk orders through group leaders.

Our Innovation

Our proposed approach combines artisan group management, democratic leader selection, product showcasing and transparent payment distribution in one platform.
