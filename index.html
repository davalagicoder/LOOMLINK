About LOOMLINK

Preserving tradition. Creating opportunities.

Many traditional artisans have excellent skills but face difficulties reaching customers and finding reliable markets for their handmade products.

LOOMLINK aims to bridge this gap by connecting artisans, artisan groups, shopkeepers and bulk buyers through one digital platform.

Our goal is to support local craftsmanship, improve market access and help traditional art reach a wider audience.
