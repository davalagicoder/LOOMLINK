About Our Project

Traditional artisans and weavers often face limited market access, dependence on middlemen, and difficulties finding bulk buyers. LOOMLINK aims to bridge this gap through a collaborative digital marketplace.


Our vision is to empower artisan communities, support traditional craftsmanship, and create better opportunities through technology.

Our Key Features

👤 User Registration

Separate roles for artisans, group leaders, and shopkeepers.

🤝 Artisan Groups

Artisans can form groups and collaborate on orders.

🗳️ Democratic Elections

Group members can nominate and elect their leaders.

🧵 Product Marketplace

Showcase handmade products and connect with buyers.

📦 Bulk Orders

Shopkeepers can request products in larger quantities.

💰 Payment Transparency

A proposed payment model makes group distribution clear.
