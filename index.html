About LOOMLINK

Preserving traditions. Creating opportunities.

LOOMLINK is a proposed digital marketplace that connects traditional artisans, weavers, artisan groups, shopkeepers and bulk buyers on one platform.

Many skilled artisans struggle to reach larger markets, find regular customers and coordinate bulk orders. LOOMLINK aims to reduce these barriers by providing a shared digital platform.

Our vision is to make traditional craftsmanship more visible, accessible and sustainable.

Our Vision

Build a connected marketplace where traditional craftsmanship receives wider recognition.

Our Mission

Help artisan communities organize their work, display products and connect with buyers.

Our Goal

Improve market access and encourage the preservation of traditional skills.

Problem & Proposed Solution

Understanding the challenges faced by artisan communities

Problems Faced by Artisans

  • Limited access to larger markets.
  • Dependence on intermediaries.
  • Difficulty finding bulk buyers.
  • Unorganized work coordination.
  • Limited digital visibility.

Our Proposed Solution

  • Digital product marketplace.
  • Artisan group management.
  • Direct buyer connections.
  • Bulk order coordination.
  • Transparent payment planning.

How LOOMLINK Works

A simple journey from artisan creation to customer connection.

01. Artisan Registration

Artisans join the platform and create their profiles.

02. Group Formation

Artisans organize themselves into working groups.

03. Product Listing

Artisans display their handmade products.

04. Buyer Connection

Shopkeepers and customers explore products and submit order enquiries.

05. Production & Distribution

Groups coordinate production, quality checking and order fulfilment.
